Everything about your account lives on one screen: **Account & settings**, from
the sidebar footer or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> → “settings”.

## Identity

Your name, avatar and handle come from GitHub and change when you change them
there. We hold no separate profile.

## GitHub connection

Shows the account you signed in with, the spaces we created, and the grant
itself. If the grant has expired you'll see a calm banner asking to reconnect —
the button says *Yes, please*.

## Your data

Links straight to your repositories on github.com. One tab on github.com is all
it takes to check our work.

## Preferences

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | Steel blue (default) or Forest. Recolours the accent everywhere. |
| **Date format** | Day-first, month-first, or ISO. |
| **Time format** | 12-hour or 24-hour. |
| **Timezone** | Follows your device, or pick one. |

The theme is a single accent token: the wordmark, active nav, checkboxes,
links, buttons and the logo mark all follow it. Paper and ink never change —
commit.moi is warm paper by design, and there is no dark mode.

:::note
These docs carry the same switch, in the bottom of the sidebar. Pick a theme
here and the manual matches your app.
:::

## Signing out

**Sign out** ends the session in this browser. It does not touch your data, and
it does not revoke anything — see
[Your data & GitHub](/your-data-and-github/#leaving) for that.

:::tip[Delete the app, not the truth]
Leaving takes one click — revoke the grant. Your tasks don't even notice.
:::
