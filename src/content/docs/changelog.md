## 2026-07 — Spaces everywhere

- **Ten space types.** Cookbook, journal, notes, contacts, finance and more —
  each a repository with screens of its own.
- **Space sharing.** Invite a collaborator to one space without touching the
  rest.
- **Docs.** This manual, at docs.commit.moi.

## 2026-06 — Keyboard-first

- **Command palette.** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> for actions, navigation, tasks
  and projects.
- **Row keys.** <kbd>↵</kbd> to open, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>↵</kbd> to complete,
  <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>↵</kbd> to move to Today.
- **Docked Task Detail.** On wide screens the panel sits beside the list
  instead of covering it.

## 2026-05 — Calm sync

- **Optimistic writes.** Tasks appear instantly; the GitHub write happens
  behind them, with a pulse while in flight and a clear marker if it fails.
- **Roll-back on failure.** A refused write never leaves a phantom task.
- **Overdue treatment.** One red **Overdue** label instead of three red things.

## 2026-04 — Email capture

- **assistant@commit.moi.** Forward anything and it becomes a task, routed to
  the space it belongs to.
- **Forest theme.** A second accent, switchable in settings.

## 2026-03 — First release

- **GitHub is the only database.** Tasks are issues, areas are labels, projects
  are milestones, the logbook is closed issues.
- **Six views.** Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Anytime, Someday, Logbook.
- **Free forever.**
