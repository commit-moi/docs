commit.moi has no database. Your tasks are issues, your spaces are private
repositories, and both live in your own GitHub account. We ship the frontend.
You already own the backend.

## What lives where

| Thing | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| Tasks, notes, projects, areas | Your private repositories |
| History of every change | Git history in those repositories |
| Your identity | Your GitHub account |
| Your access token | Your browser session |
| Anything else | Nowhere. There is no anything else. |

<Aside type="note" title="One tab is all it takes">
Open the repository on github.com and compare it to the app. Trust isn't a
promise here.
</Aside>

## What we ask GitHub for

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Private repositories" icon="lock">
    To create your spaces and read and write the issues inside them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your profile" icon="user">
    Name, avatar and the email on your account — for the sidebar and email capture.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Nothing else is requested, and nothing outside the spaces we created is
touched. Repositories you already had are none of our business.

## Leaving

There is no export, because there is nothing of yours in here to export.

1. Revoke the OAuth grant in your GitHub settings.
2. That's it. Your repositories, issues and history stay exactly as they are.

<Aside type="tip" title="Cancel commit.moi. Keep everything.">
If commit.moi disappeared tomorrow, nothing happens to your tasks. They're
issues in your repos.
</Aside>
